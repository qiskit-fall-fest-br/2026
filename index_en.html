Quantum Brasil and CESAR teamed up with IBM to organize the Brazilian edition of the Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, which will be both online and in person!

Join online, in person, or both.

The in-person side of the 2026 event is being organized with the support of LACQ Feynman and 5 quantum computing academic leagues and groups, spread across different regions of Brazil:

Quantum CESAR

CESAR's research and development group in quantum computing, focused on applied research, training and business, including quantum machine learning, combinatorial optimization and education.

Nov 4 · 2:30pm-6pm
Av. Cais do Apolo, 77, Room 004 - Recife, PE

Quanta Tech USP

USP's Academic League of Quantum Computing, with students across 3 campuses and a structured training track to work across several fronts of quantum technologies and spread that knowledge.

Nov 6 and 13 · 3pm-4:30pm
Rua Arlindo Béttio, Ermelino Matarazzo - São Paulo, SP

LACQ Belém

Based at UFPA's LabTIC, it works to spread knowledge and scientific training in quantum computing, strengthening the role of Brazil's North region in quantum technologies.

Nov 10-13 · 8:30am-12pm and 2pm-4pm
Rua da Reitoria, Guamá - Belém, PA

LACIQ UFPE

Founded in 2023, it was Brazil's first academic league focused on quantum technologies. It works on quantum software, information, communication and machine learning, and runs UFPE's Quantum Computing Symposium and HackaQuantum.

Nov 13 · 1pm-5pm
Av. Jornalista Aníbal Fernandes, s/n, Cidade Universitária - Recife, PE

LACQ UFRGS

A study group of UFRGS students, mainly from the Institute of Informatics and the Institute of Physics, aiming to deepen and produce new knowledge in quantum computing.

Nov 24-25 · 9am-12pm and 1pm-5pm
Av. Bento Gonçalves, 9500, Agronomia - Porto Alegre, RS

Speakers and Instructors

Alexandre Pfeifer

Business Leader for Quantum Computing in Latin America at IBM since 2024. Previously, he was responsible for establishing IBM Research's research collaborations and technology licensing in Brazil, Latin America, and with global strategic clients. He holds a Computer Engineering degree from Unicamp and an MBA from the University of Chicago's Booth School of Business.

Marcos Reballo

With a background in aeronautical engineering, he is a researcher focused on Quantum Machine Learning, with several published papers in quantum computing. A specialist in Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQAs) and hybrid ML architectures, he teaches the variational circuits class at Qiskit Fall Fest 2026.

Jefferson Deyvis

Jefferson Deyvis dos Santos Silva is a researcher at CESAR and a Quantum Computing specialist. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from CIn-UFPE, plus a master's and bachelor's degree in Physics. He works on quantum circuit optimization and Quantum Machine Learning, applying these technologies to innovation projects, including strategic research with FEBRABAN.

Deyvison Conrado

Deyvison M. Conrado is a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at UFPE, focusing on Quantum Communications, with a master's in Physical Engineering and a degree in Electrical Engineering from UFRPE. He works as a Quantum Computing tutor at CESAR, dedicated to teaching and researching Quantum Machine Learning (QML) solutions for the business and financial sectors.

Naomy Duarte Gomes

PhD in Physics, master's in Physics Applied to Medicine and Biology, and an MBA in Data Science from USP, with a postdoctorate in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from the same university. She has experience in Optics, Complex Systems and Quantum Optimization. She was an Eiffel Excellence Scholarship recipient at the Institut d'Optique in France, and works as a Data Scientist at CESAR.

Otto Pires

Otto Menegasso Pires holds a bachelor's in Computer Science and a master's in Computational Intelligence from UFSC, and is currently a PhD candidate in Computational Modeling and Industrial Technology at SENAI CIMATEC, focusing on Quantum Machine Learning. He works as a researcher in Quantum Technologies at SENAI CIMATEC, on applied research projects aimed at digital transformation and industrial innovation.
