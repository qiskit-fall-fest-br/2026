Quantum Brasil and CESAR teamed up with IBM to organize the Brazilian edition of the Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, which will be both online and in person!

Join online, in person, or both.

The in-person side of the event is being organized with the support of LACQ Feynman and the following leagues, institutions and universities:

•CESAR •QUASAR (UFPB) •LACQ UEA •LACQ USP •LACQ UTFPR •LACQ PUC RS •LACQ UFRGS •LACQ - Belém •LACQ UNISO •LACICA Universidade do Vale do Rio doce •UPE •UFPE

Play the Entanglion board game in person (developed by IBM) and watch talks, classes and workshops on quantum computing, online.

For students, IT professionals, science enthusiasts, and anyone interested in exploring the future of quantum computing.

Talks and hands-on activities on programming quantum computers.
